  reading these: the MEAN of staff quality is the wrong test, and
  an earlier version of this function used it and found nothing —
  a set holding both the highest and lowest staff averages back to
  the middle. Extremity is the hypothesis, because Savant scores
  only pitches near the zone edge, so a catcher whose staff locates
  unusually well or badly has an unusual share of his takes inside
  the window they evaluate. Sensitivity to run-value weighting
  points instead at the two methods valuing counts differently.
